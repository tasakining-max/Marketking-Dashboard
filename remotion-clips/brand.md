# Tasaki Air Brand Colors

| Name        | Hex       | Usage                        |
|-------------|-----------|------------------------------|
| Tasaki Blue | `#0074A2` | Primary / Logo               |
| White       | `#FFFFFF` | Background / Base            |
| Light Gray  | `#F5F5F5` | Surface / Section            |
| Sky Tint    | `#E8F4FA` | Accent Background            |
| Dark Blue   | `#005A82` | Hover / Emphasis             |

## Brand Background Gradient
ใช้บ่อยในสื่อโซเชียล (โพสต์, รีลส์)

```css
background: linear-gradient(160deg, #E8F4FA 0%, #0074A2 40%, #005A82 100%);
```

| Stop  | Hex       | Position |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Light | `#E8F4FA` | 0%       |
| Mid   | `#0074A2` | 40%      |
| Dark  | `#005A82` | 100%     |
